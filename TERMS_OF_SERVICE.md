## 4. Alerts, server settings, and restock guesses

**Personal alerts.** You can request a one-time Discord direct message for an eligible fruit when it next appears in verified stock. Delivery depends on the tracker seeing the rotation and on Discord allowing the bot to send you a message. Alerts may be paused if direct messages are unavailable. Use `/fruit-alerts list` to see your alerts and `/fruit-alerts remove` to cancel one. Use the `clear` option in `/goal` to remove a saved savings goal.

**Server announcements.** An authorized server administrator can set a destination channel, configure a watchlist, and choose a role for mentions. Server administrators are responsible for selecting suitable channels, reviewing channel access and role permissions, and making sure automated posts and mentions follow their community’s rules. Discord permissions, service interruptions, and incomplete stock observations can affect delivery. Use `/stock-alerts disable` to stop automated announcements.

**Restock guesses.** In a server, you can choose one eligible rotating fruit per dealer for an upcoming round while entries are open. Scores are based on the bot’s verified observations. Rounds with insufficient data can go unscored. Leaderboards and verified round summaries may display your Discord account and result to people who can view those posts. Participation is free and earns points for the server leaderboard.
